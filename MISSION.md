# Mission: Pass HSK1 in 3 Months

## Goal
Pass the HSK1 (Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi Level 1) Chinese proficiency exam within 3 months.

## Context
- **Starting Level**: Complete beginner (no prior Chinese study)
- **Target Exam**: HSK1 
- **Timeline**: 3 months
- **Why It Matters**: [To be determined - user can clarify if they have a specific purpose]

## HSK1 Requirements
- ~150 core vocabulary words
- Pinyin mastery and tone system
- Character recognition and basic writing
- Simple conversational phrases
- Listening comprehension at beginner level
- Reading comprehension of basic texts

## Success Criteria
- Pass the HSK1 exam (150-300 points typically needed for minimum pass)
- Comfortable with basic daily greetings and introductions
- Able to read and write ~100-150 characters
- Understanding of tone system and proper pronunciation

## Approach
- Daily micro-lessons (15-30 min each) with progressive difficulty
- Focus on high-frequency vocabulary first
- Regular spaced retrieval practice to build memory strength
- Listening and speaking integrated early
- Mock exams and practice tests in final month
