Tone 4: Falling (`)

Key Characteristics:
  • Pitch: HIGH, falls DOWN quickly
  • Visual: ╲ (falling line)
  • Symbol: à è ì ò ù
  • Feeling: Like giving a strong command or emphasis
  • Tone Mark: Grave accent (`) over the vowel

Understanding Tone 4

Tone 4 is actually quite easy because:

  • It's very distinct from the other tones
  • It feels natural in English (like giving a command)
  • Most English speakers naturally use this pitch contour
  • It's the easiest tone to recognize by ear

How to Produce Tone 4

  1. Start HIGH: Begin at a naturally high pitch
  2. Fall FAST: Quickly drop down in pitch
  3. End LOW: Finish at a low note
  4. Think "command": Like saying "Stop!" in English
  5. Practice repeatedly: This one should feel natural quickly!

Practice Exercises

🎯Exercise 1: Tone 4 Recognition

Which sounds are Tone 4? (Look for the falling pattern)

  • Sound A: High, falls quickly → ✓ Tone 4
  • Sound B: Stays high → ✗ Tone 1
  • Sound C: High, falls quickly → ✓ Tone 4
  • Sound D: Dips low → ✗ Tone 3
  • Sound E: High, falls quickly → ✓ Tone 4

🎤Exercise 2: All Tones Together

Say the "MA" family (all 4 tones) in order:

  1. mā - High, flat (Tone 1)
  2. má - Rising (Tone 2)
  3. mǎ - Dipping (Tone 3)
  4. mà - Falling (Tone 4)

Do this 10 times! You should hear 4 completely different sounds.

🔄Exercise 3: Random Tone Identification

Your brain can now identify tones! Try this:

  1. Imagine hearing a random tone (1, 2, 3, or 4)
  2. Guess which tone it is before listening
  3. Listen to confirm
  4. Repeat 20 times

This trains your ear to distinguish tones unconsciously!
