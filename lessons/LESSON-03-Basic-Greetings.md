## The 10 Essential Greetings for HSK1

### 1️⃣ 你好 (nǐ hǎo) - Hello

**Meaning:** Hello / Hi (formal/semi-formal)

**Breakdown:**
- 你 (nǐ) = you
- 好 (hǎo) = good
- Together: "You good" → Hello

**Pronunciation:** nee-HOW (tone 3, then tone 3)

**Tone Practice:**
- 你 = Tone 3 (dip low)
- 好 = Tone 3 (dip low)
- Together: Create a conversation flow

**When to Use:**
- Meeting someone for the first time
- Greeting in morning/afternoon
- Polite, standard greeting

**Response:**
- 你好 (same response back)
- 你好，很高兴认识你 (Hello, nice to meet you)

**Audio Note:** This is THE most important greeting. Practice 50 times!

---

### 2️⃣ 谢谢 (xièxie) - Thank you

**Meaning:** Thank you / Thanks

**Breakdown:**
- 谢 (xiè) = to thank
- 谢 (xiè) = repeated for emphasis
- Together: Thank you very much

**Pronunciation:** SYEH-syeh (tone 4, then tone 4)

**Tone Practice:**
- Both characters are Tone 4 (fall down quickly)
- Should sound like you're giving a command

**When to Use:**
- Someone helps you
- Someone gives you something
- Expressing gratitude

**Response:**
- 不客气 (búkèqi) = You're welcome
- 没关系 (méi guānxi) = It's nothing

**Variation:** 谢谢你 (xièxie nǐ) = Thank you (to you, more personal)

---

### 3️⃣ 对不起 (duìbúqǐ) - I'm sorry / Excuse me

**Meaning:** Sorry / Excuse me / I apologize

**Breakdown:**
- 对 (duì) = correct/towards
- 不 (bú) = not  
- 起 (qǐ) = rise up
- Together: "Not right"

**Pronunciation:** dway-boo-CHEE (tone 4, tone 2, tone 3)

**When to Use:**
- You made a mistake
- You need to interrupt someone
- You're passing through a crowd ("Excuse me")
- You're late to a meeting

**Response:**
- 没关系 (méi guānxi) = It's okay, no problem
- 不要紧 (búyào jǐn) = Never mind

---

### 4️⃣ 再见 (zàijiàn) - Goodbye

**Meaning:** Goodbye / See you again

**Breakdown:**
- 再 (zài) = again / next time
- 见 (jiàn) = see
- Together: "See you again"

**Pronunciation:** zye-JEN (tone 4, then tone 4)

**When to Use:**
- Leaving a conversation
- Ending a phone call
- Saying goodbye to friends/colleagues

**Similar phrases:**
- 拜拜 (báibai) = Bye bye (casual, with friends)
- 一会儿见 (yī huǐ r jiàn) = See you in a bit

---

### 5️⃣ 好的 (hǎo de) - Okay / Sure / Alright

**Meaning:** Okay / Sure / I agree / That's good

**Breakdown:**
- 好 (hǎo) = good
- 的 (de) = particle (no meaning, just softens the word)

**Pronunciation:** HOW-duh (tone 3, then neutral tone)

**When to Use:**
- Someone asks you to do something → "Okay!"
- Someone suggests something → "Sure!"
- Agreement/confirmation

**Tone Note:** The second character 的 is neutral tone (light, quick)

---

### 6️⃣ 你好吗？(nǐ hǎo ma?) - How are you?

**Meaning:** How are you? / How are you doing?

**Breakdown:**
- 你好 (nǐ hǎo) = hello
- 吗 (ma) = question particle
- Together: "You good?" → How are you?

**Pronunciation:** nee-HOW-ma? (tone 3, tone 3, neutral)

**When to Use:**
- After greeting someone
- Showing care/interest
- Starting a conversation

**Common Response:**
- 我很好 (wǒ hěn hǎo) = I'm very good / I'm fine
- 还不错 (háibúcuò) = Pretty good / Not bad

**Tone Note:** The 吗 (ma) makes it a question - very light, neutral tone

---

### 7️⃣ 谢谢你 (xièxie nǐ) - Thank you (to you)

**Meaning:** Thank you / Thank you very much

**Breakdown:**
- 谢谢 (xièxie) = thanks
- 你 (nǐ) = you
- Together: Thanks (to) you

**Pronunciation:** SYEH-syeh nee (tone 4, tone 4, tone 3)

**When to Use:**
- More personal/intimate than just 谢谢
- Someone specific did something for you
- Extra gratitude

**Variation:** 非常谢谢你 (fēicháng xièxie nǐ) = Thank you very much (very formal)

---

### 8️⃣ 不客气 (búkèqi) - You're welcome

**Meaning:** You're welcome / No problem / Don't mention it

**Breakdown:**
- 不 (bú) = not
- 客气 (kèqi) = polite/formal
- Together: "Not polite" → "Don't be formal with me"

**Pronunciation:** boo-KUH-chee (tone 2, then tone 4)

**When to Use:**
- Someone thanks you
- Brushing off their thanks
- Being friendly/informal

**Tone Note:** First character 不 is usually Tone 2 when before a Tone 4, otherwise Tone 4

---

### 9️⃣ 请 (qǐng) - Please

**Meaning:** Please / May I ask... / Would you...

**Breakdown:**
- 请 (qǐng) = to request / invite
- A single character meaning "please"

**Pronunciation:** CHING (tone 3)

**When to Use:**
- Making a polite request
- Inviting someone
- Starting a polite question

**Examples:**
- 请坐 (qǐng zuò) = Please sit
- 请喝水 (qǐng hē shuǐ) = Please drink water
- 请告诉我 (qǐng gàosu wǒ) = Please tell me

---

### 🔟 我很好 (wǒ hěn hǎo) - I'm very good / I'm fine

**Meaning:** I'm very well / I'm fine / I'm doing great

**Breakdown:**
- 我 (wǒ) = I / me
- 很 (hěn) = very
- 好 (hǎo) = good

**Pronunciation:** wuh HUN HOW (tone 3, tone 3, tone 3)

**When to Use:**
- Answering "How are you?"
- Expressing contentment
- Responding positively

**Variations:**
- 还不错 (háibúcuò) = Not bad / Pretty good
- 非常好 (fēicháng hǎo) = Very very good
- 很开心 (hěn kāixīn) = Very happy

## Simple Greeting Dialogue

### Scenario: Meeting Someone at a Cafe

```
Person A: 你好！(nǐ hǎo!)
         Hello!

Person B: 你好！(nǐ hǎo!)
         Hello!

Person A: 你好吗？(nǐ hǎo ma?)
         How are you?

Person B: 我很好。你呢？(wǒ hěn hǎo, nǐ ne?)
         I'm fine. How about you?

Person A: 我也很好。(wǒ yě hěn hǎo)
         I'm also very well.

Person A: 谢谢！(xièxie!)
         Thanks!

Person B: 不客气！(búkèqi!)
         You're welcome!

Both: 再见！(zàijiàn!)
      Goodbye!
```

## Pronunciation Practice

### Key Tones in Greetings

| Greeting | Tone Pattern | Difficulty |
|----------|--------------|------------|
| 你好 | 3-3 | Easy (both same) |
| 谢谢 | 4-4 | Easy (both same) |
| 对不起 | 4-2-3 | Medium (three different) |
| 再见 | 4-4 | Easy (both same) |
| 好的 | 3-neutral | Easy |
| 请 | 3 | Very easy (one syllable) |

### Tone Sandwich Practice

Since 你好 uses Tone 3 twice, they connect:
1. **First 你:** Go down (tone 3)
2. **Second 好:** Go up slightly from where the first ended
3. **Together:** Creates a natural rhythm

**Practice:** Say "你好" 20 times, focusing on the flow between characters

## Tone Correction Guide

If people don't understand you, it's usually a tone issue. Check these:

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| 你 doesn't sound like you're asking | Dip lower with Tone 3 |
| 再 sounds like you're falling | Yes! That's Tone 4, correct |
| 吗 is too loud | Make it light and quick (neutral) |

## Cultural Notes

### Greeting Etiquette in Chinese
1. **Handshake:** Light handshake (not too firm)
2. **Eye Contact:** Yes, show respect
3. **Formality:** 你好 is always safe (more formal than casual)
4. **Honorifics:** Learn names and titles early

### Regional Variations
- **Northern China:** 你好 is most common
- **Southern China:** More casual greetings used
- **Taiwan:** More formal, 您 (formal "you") is used
