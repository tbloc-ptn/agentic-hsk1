## Chinese Sentence Structure: The Basics

### The SVO Pattern (Subject-Verb-Object)

Chinese follows a **Subject-Verb-Object** pattern, just like English!

**Pattern:** Subject + Verb + Object

### Example Breakdown

**Sentence:** 我吃饭
- **我** (wǒ) = I [Subject]
- **吃** (chī) = eat [Verb]
- **饭** (fàn) = rice/food [Object]
- **English:** "I eat food" / "I eat rice"

**Notice:** Same word order as English!

### Simple Formula

```
[Person] + [Action] + [Thing]
I        + eat     + rice
我      + 吃      + 饭
```

## The 5 Essential Verbs for HSK1

### Verb 1: 是 (shì) - "To Be"

**Meaning:** To be / Am / Is / Are

**Tone:** Tone 4 (falling)

**Pronunciation:** "shuh"

**Structure:** Subject + 是 + Noun

**Examples:**
- 我是学生 (wǒ shì xuésheng) = I am a student
- 他是老师 (tā shì lǎoshī) = He is a teacher
- 你是医生吗？(nǐ shì yīshēng ma?) = Are you a doctor?

**Response Format:**
- 是 (shì) = Yes (short answer)
- 不是 (búshì) = No (short answer)

**Negation:**
- 我不是学生 (wǒ búshì xuésheng) = I am not a student

**Key Point:** 不 (not) comes BEFORE the verb

---

### Verb 2: 有 (yǒu) - "To Have"

**Meaning:** To have / To own / There is/are

**Tone:** Tone 3 (dipping)

**Pronunciation:** "yo"

**Structure:** Subject + 有 + Object

**Examples:**
- 我有一个朋友 (wǒ yǒu yī ge péngyou) = I have a friend
- 他有三个妹妹 (tā yǒu sān ge mèimei) = He has three younger sisters
- 你有手机吗？(nǐ yǒu shǒujī ma?) = Do you have a phone?

**Response:**
- 有 (yǒu) = Yes, I do
- 没有 (méiyǒu) = No, I don't

**Negation:**
- 我没有手机 (wǒ méiyǒu shǒujī) = I don't have a phone
- **NOT:** 我不有 (wrong!)

**Key Point:** Use 没有 for negation, not 不有

---

### Verb 3: 吃 (chī) - "To Eat"

**Meaning:** To eat / To consume food

**Tone:** Tone 1 (high and flat)

**Pronunciation:** "chuh" (like "cheese" without the z)

**Structure:** Subject + 吃 + Food

**Examples:**
- 我吃饭 (wǒ chī fàn) = I eat rice / I eat food
- 他吃苹果 (tā chī píngguǒ) = He eats an apple
- 你吃什么？(nǐ chī shénme?) = What do you eat?
- 我吃米饭 (wǒ chī mǐfàn) = I eat rice

**Compound Examples:**
- 我早上吃面包 (wǒ zǎoshang chī miànbāo) = I eat bread in the morning
- 他们吃中餐 (tāmen chī zhōngcān) = They eat Chinese food

**Negation:**
- 我不吃肉 (wǒ búchī ròu) = I don't eat meat
- 她没有吃过日本菜 (tā méiyǒu chīguò Rìběn cài) = She has never eaten Japanese food

---

### Verb 4: 喝 (hē) - "To Drink"

**Meaning:** To drink / To consume liquids

**Tone:** Tone 1 (high)

**Pronunciation:** "huh" (like the English "huh?" but higher)

**Structure:** Subject + 喝 + Beverage

**Examples:**
- 我喝水 (wǒ hē shuǐ) = I drink water
- 他喝茶 (tā hē chá) = He drinks tea
- 你喝咖啡吗？(nǐ hē kāfēi ma?) = Do you drink coffee?
- 我不喝酒 (wǒ búhē jiǔ) = I don't drink alcohol

**Common Beverages:**
- 水 (shuǐ) = water
- 茶 (chá) = tea
- 咖啡 (kāfēi) = coffee
- 牛奶 (niúnǎi) = milk
- 果汁 (guǒzhī) = juice
- 酒 (jiǔ) = alcohol/wine

**Negation:**
- 我不喝咖啡 (wǒ búhē kāfēi) = I don't drink coffee

---

### Verb 5: 去 (qù) - "To Go"

**Meaning:** To go / To travel to / To head to

**Tone:** Tone 4 (falling)

**Pronunciation:** "choo"

**Structure:** Subject + 去 + Location / Subject + 去 + Verb

**Examples:**
- 我去学校 (wǒ qù xuéxiào) = I go to school
- 他去北京 (tā qù Běijīng) = He goes to Beijing
- 你去哪里？(nǐ qù nǎli?) = Where are you going?
- 我去看电影 (wǒ qù kàn diànyǐng) = I go watch a movie

**Two Uses:**
1. **Go to a place:** 去 + Location
   - 我去医院 (wǒ qù yīyuàn) = I go to the hospital
   
2. **Go to do something:** 去 + Action
   - 我去睡觉 (wǒ qù shuìjiào) = I go to sleep
   - 我去买菜 (wǒ qù mǎi cài) = I go shopping for vegetables

**Negation:**
- 我不去 (wǒ búqù) = I'm not going

## Building Simple Sentences

### Formula 1: Subject + 是 + Identity

```
我    是    学生
I     am    a student
```

**Your Practice:**
- 我是... (nǐ shì...) = I am... [fill in your job/role]
- 他是医生 = He is a doctor
- 她是律师 = She is a lawyer

### Formula 2: Subject + 有 + Object

```
我    有    一个朋友
I     have  one friend
```

**Your Practice:**
- 我有... = I have... [brother, book, phone, etc.]
- 他有三个朋友 = He has 3 friends

### Formula 3: Subject + 吃/喝 + Object

```
我    吃    米饭
I     eat   rice
```

**Your Practice:**
- 我喝... = I drink... [water, tea, etc.]
- 他吃... = He eats... [apple, bread, etc.]

### Formula 4: Subject + 去 + Place

```
我    去    学校
I     go    to school
```

**Your Practice:**
- 我去... = I go to... [hospital, park, Beijing, etc.]

## Combining Verbs (More Advanced)

### Pattern: Subject + Verb1 + Verb2 + Object

**Example:** 我去吃饭 (wǒ qù chī fàn)
- 我 = I
- 去 = go
- 吃 = eat
- 饭 = food
- **Translation:** "I go eat food" = "I go out to eat"

**Other Examples:**
- 我去喝茶 (wǒ qù hē chá) = I go have tea
- 他去看医生 (tā qù kàn yīshēng) = He goes to see a doctor
- 她去买衣服 (tā qù mǎi yīfu) = She goes to buy clothes

## Verb Conjugation (It's Simpler Than You Think!)

### Great News: No Conjugation!

Chinese verbs DON'T change based on person or tense!

| English | Chinese | Same Word! |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| I eat | 我吃 | 吃 |
| You eat | 你吃 | 吃 |
| He eats | 他吃 | 吃 |
| We eat | 我们吃 | 吃 |
| They eat | 他们吃 | 吃 |

**Instead, use time markers:**
- 今天 (jīntiān) = today
- 昨天 (zuótiān) = yesterday
- 明天 (míngtiān) = tomorrow
- 现在 (xiànzài) = now

**Example:**
- 今天我吃米饭 (jīntiān wǒ chī mǐfàn) = Today I eat rice
- 昨天我吃了面包 (zuótiān wǒ chīle miànbāo) = Yesterday I ate bread

## Question Formation

### Adding 吗 (ma) - The Question Particle

To make a statement into a question, add 吗 at the end:

**Statement:** 你有朋友
**Question:** 你有朋友吗？

| Statement | Question | Translation |
|-----------|----------|-------------|
| 他是医生 | 他是医生吗？ | Is he a doctor? |
| 我有手机 | 我有手机吗？ | Do I have a phone? |
| 你吃米饭 | 你吃米饭吗？ | Do you eat rice? |
| 他去学校 | 他去学校吗？ | Is he going to school? |

**Note:** No word order change needed! Just add 吗 at the end.

## Negation Patterns

### Pattern 1: Verb Negation (Most Common)
```
Subject + 不 + Verb + Object
我      不   吃    肉
I       do not eat meat
```

**Examples:**
- 我不是医生 (wǒ búshì yīshēng) = I'm not a doctor
- 他不吃肉 (tā búchī ròu) = He doesn't eat meat
- 我不去 (wǒ búqù) = I'm not going

### Pattern 2: Special Case - 有 (to have)
Use 没有 (méiyǒu) instead of 不有:

```
Subject + 没有 + Object
我      没有    朋友
I       don't have  friends
```

**Never say:** 我不有朋友 ❌  
**Always say:** 我没有朋友 ✅

## Key Takeaways

✅ Chinese uses SVO word order (same as English)  
✅ Master 5 verbs: 是, 有, 吃, 喝, 去  
✅ Verbs don't conjugate (same form for all persons)  
✅ Use 不 for negation (but 没有 for 有)  
✅ Add 吗 at the end to make questions  
✅ Combine verbs naturally: 我去吃饭
