## What are Radicals?

**Radical** (部首, bùshǒu) = the "root" or "building block" of a Chinese character.

### Think of it like this:
- **English:** Words are made from letters (a-z) → 26 building blocks
- **Chinese:** Characters are made from radicals → ~214 building blocks

### Example: The Radical 水 (Water)

The water radical (水, shuǐ) appears in words about water:

| Character | Radical | Meaning | Reading |
|-----------|---------|---------|---------|
| 水 | 水 | Water | shuǐ |
| 河 | 水 | River | hé |
| 海 | 水 | Sea | hǎi |
| 湖 | 水 | Lake | hú |
| 泳 | 水 | Swim | yǒng |

**Notice:** All these characters contain the water radical (水), and they all relate to water!

---

## Why Learn Radicals?

### 1. **Meaning Clues**
- Radical often hints at the character's meaning
- 水 (water) → characters are water-related
- 木 (wood) → characters are wood/tree-related
- 心 (heart) → characters are emotion-related

### 2. **Memory Aid**
- Characters are easier to remember when grouped by radical
- You learn patterns instead of memorizing random strokes

### 3. **Dictionary Lookup**
- Chinese dictionaries organize characters by radical
- Finding a character = find its radical first

### 4. **Character Recognition**
- ~50 radicals cover 95% of common characters
- Master 10-15 radicals → recognize thousands of characters

## How Radicals Appear in Characters

Radicals can appear in different positions:

### Position 1: LEFT SIDE
```
person (亻) + other strokes = new character
亻+ 他 = 他 (he)
```

### Position 2: RIGHT SIDE
```
water (氵) + other strokes = new character
氵 + 去 = 泣 (cry)
```

### Position 3: TOP
```
roof (宀) + other strokes = new character
宀 + 子 = 家 (home)
```

### Position 4: BOTTOM
```
fire (灬) + other strokes = new character
火 + 八 = 灬 (flames)
```

## How to Use Radicals to Remember Characters

### Method: The "Radical + Meaning" Trick

**Example: Learn 河 (river)**

1. **Identify the radical:** 河 = 氵 (water) + 可
2. **Understand the radical:** 氵means water-related
3. **Create connection:** "River is made of water" = 河 is water-related
4. **Remember:** Next time you see 氵, think "water-related"

### Real-World Example: Learn the "Family"

**The 心 (Heart) Family = Emotions:**

| Character | Breakdown | Meaning | Memory |
|-----------|-----------|---------|--------|
| 心 | 心 alone | Heart | The core emotion |
| 想 | 心 + 想 | Think/Want | What your heart wants |
| 感 | 心 + 感 | Feel | What your heart feels |
| 怕 | 心 + 怕 | Afraid | Fear comes from heart |

**See the pattern?** All emotions are tied to the heart radical!

## 214 Radicals? Don't Panic!

You don't need to memorize all 214 radicals immediately!

### Learning Path:
- **Week 1-2:** Master 5 radicals (人, 木, 水, 火, 心)
- **Week 3-4:** Learn 10 more (金, 口, 日, 月, etc.)
- **Month 2:** You'll naturally pick up 20-30 through exposure
- **Month 3:** 50 radicals = 80% of common characters
