Tone 1: High Flat (¯)

Key Characteristics:
  • Pitch: HIGH and STAYS HIGH
  • Visual: ─── (straight line)
  • Symbol: ā ē ī ō ū
  • Feeling: Like a siren that doesn't change pitch

Understanding Tone 1 Visually

Imagine your voice pitch as a graph. Tone 1 looks like this:

Perfectly flat and high!

How to Produce Tone 1

  1. Find your high pitch: Say "oooooh" at a naturally high pitch (not strained)
  2. Keep it flat: Make sure it doesn't go up or down
  3. Release and relax: Let your voice naturally fall when you're done
  4. Practice repeatedly: Say it 10+ times until it feels natural

Tone 1 vs. All Tones

Now that you understand Tone 1, here's how it compares to the other tones:

Tone 1 (High): mā - stays high, never changes

Tone 2 (Rising): má - goes UP (opposite of 1)

Tone 3 (Dipping): mǎ - goes DOWN then UP

Tone 4 (Falling): mà - goes DOWN quickly

The key difference:

  • Tone 1 is the most stable - it doesn't move
  • All other tones move - that's what makes them different
  • Once you master Tone 1, the others become clearer!
