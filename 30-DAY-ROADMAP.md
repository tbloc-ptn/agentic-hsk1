# HSK1 30-Day Roadmap: Foundation Phase

## Overview
This 30-day plan covers **Phase 1: Foundation** (Weeks 1-4) of your 3-month HSK1 journey. Your goal: master pinyin, tones, and recognize your first 40 characters.

**Daily Commitment:** 30-45 minutes  
**Start Date:** 2026-09-11  
**End Date:** 2026-10-11

---

## Week 1: Pinyin Mastery (Days 1-7)
**Goal:** Master all 4 tones; identify tones by sound; write pinyin with tone marks

### Day 1: Introduction to Pinyin & The Four Tones
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Watch: [Mandarin.com - Pinyin & Tones Intro](https://www.mandarin.com) (5 min)
  - Read: Lesson 1 notes (understand tone categories)
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Listen: ChinesePod Newbie - Tones 101 (10 min)
  - Practice: Repeat each tone 5x aloud (mā, má, mǎ, mà)
  - Write: Pinyin for these tones in notebook
- **Anki:** Start tone flashcard deck (10 cards)

### Day 2: Tone 1 (High Flat) Deep Dive
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Listen & Repeat: 10 Tone 1 words (mā, tā, lā, nǐ, etc.)
  - Record yourself speaking
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Tone listening exercise: Identify 20 Tone 1 syllables from audio
  - Compare your recording to native speaker
  - Write 5 new Tone 1 words with pinyin
- **Anki:** 15 Tone 1 cards

### Day 3: Tone 2 (Rising) Deep Dive
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Listen & Repeat: 10 Tone 2 words (má, tá, lá, etc.)
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Tone listening: Identify 20 Tone 2 syllables
  - Compare Tone 1 vs. Tone 2 (hear the difference)
  - Shadowing: Repeat after native speaker
- **Anki:** 15 Tone 2 cards + review Tone 1 (5 cards)

### Day 4: Tone 3 (Low Dipping) Deep Dive
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Listen & Repeat: 10 Tone 3 words (mǎ, tǎ, lǎ, etc.)
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Tone listening: Identify 20 Tone 3 syllables
  - Compare all three tones so far
  - Shadowing practice
- **Anki:** 15 Tone 3 cards + mixed review (Tones 1-3, 10 cards)

### Day 5: Tone 4 (Falling) Deep Dive
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Listen & Repeat: 10 Tone 4 words (mà, tà, là, etc.)
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Tone listening: Identify 20 Tone 4 syllables
  - Compare all four tones together
  - Record yourself doing tone comparison
- **Anki:** 15 Tone 4 cards + mixed review (all 4 tones, 15 cards)

### Day 6: Tone Combinations & Tone Sandhi (Introduction)
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Review: Tone pairs (T1+T1, T1+T2, T1+T3, T1+T4)
  - Listen to tone sandhi examples
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Practice: Identify tone combinations in 30+ syllables
  - Shadowing: Read tone sandhi explanations aloud
  - Quiz: Can you identify all 4 tones?
- **Anki:** Mixed tone review (30 cards across all tones)

### Day 7: TONE MASTERY TEST & REVIEW
- **Morning (20 min):**
  - Take Mandarin.com tone listening test
  - Target score: 80%+
  - Review any weak areas
- **Evening (25 min):**
  - Record yourself pronouncing 20 random syllables with all 4 tones
  - Compare to native speaker
  - Write pinyin for 30 syllables you hear
- **Anki:** Full tone review (40 cards)
- **Milestone:** ✅ Can identify all 4 tones by sound; write pinyin with correct tone marks

## Week 2: Character Structure & Radicals (Days 8-14)
**Goal:** Learn 20 basic characters; understand 5 key radicals; practice stroke order

### Day 8: Introduction to Radicals & Character Structure
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Read: "Radicals 101" lesson
  - Understand: What radicals are and why they matter
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Hanzi Writer: Learn radical 人 (person) - 3 characters
  - Radical flashcards: Start 10 cards with 人
  - Write: Each character 3 times
- **Anki:** 10 radical flashcards

### Day 9: Radical 木 (Wood) and Related Characters
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Learn radical 木 and 3-4 characters containing it
  - Hanzi Writer: Practice stroke order
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Write each character 5 times
  - Create mnemonics (木 looks like a tree!)
  - Shadowing: Pronounce while writing
- **Anki:** 10 new radical cards + 5 previous review

### Day 10: Radical 水 (Water) and Related Characters
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Learn radical 水 and 3-4 characters
  - Hanzi Writer practice
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Write characters 5 times each
  - Review: 人 and 木 characters (write without reference)
  - Character quiz: Can you write 6 characters from memory?
- **Anki:** 10 new cards + 10 previous review

### Day 11: Radical 火 (Fire) and Related Characters
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Learn radical 火 and 3-4 characters
  - Hanzi Writer: Stroke order practice
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Write all new characters 5 times
  - Review characters from Days 8-10
  - Radical review: Can you identify 人, 木, 水?
- **Anki:** 10 new cards + 15 previous review

### Day 12: Radical 金 (Metal) and Related Characters
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Learn radical 金 and 3-4 characters
  - Hanzi Writer practice
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Write new characters 5 times
  - Comprehensive review: Write all 12-16 learned characters from memory
  - Pronunciation review: Say each character aloud
- **Anki:** 10 new cards + 20 previous review

### Day 13: Radical 心 (Heart) and Related Characters
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Learn radical 心 and 3-4 characters
  - Hanzi Writer: Stroke order
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Write characters 5 times each
  - Character recognition quiz: See 20 characters, identify them
  - Radical recognition: Identify which radical is in each character
- **Anki:** 10 new cards + 25 previous review

### Day 14: CHARACTER MASTERY TEST
- **Morning (20 min):**
  - Write test: Write 20 characters from memory (Hanzi Writer will show if correct)
  - Target: 18/20 correct
- **Evening (25 min):**
  - Recognition test: See 20 characters + pinyin, match them
  - Radical review: Identify 5 radicals in mixed characters
  - Create your own character study sheet (favorite 20 characters)
- **Anki:** Full character review (40 cards)
- **Milestone:** ✅ Recognize 20 basic characters; understand 5 key radicals; confident stroke order

## Week 3: Common Greetings & First Words (Days 15-21)
**Goal:** Know HSK1 must-know phrases; count 1-10; greet people confidently

### Day 15: Basic Greetings (你好, 谢谢, 对不起)
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Listen: Greetings in context (Easy Chinese - YouTube, 2 min clip)
  - Shadowing: Repeat native speaker (5 times each)
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Learn 5 greetings with meanings:
    - 你好 (nǐ hǎo) - Hello
    - 谢谢 (xièxie) - Thank you
    - 对不起 (duìbúqǐ) - Sorry
    - 再见 (zàijiàn) - Goodbye
    - 好的 (hǎo de) - Okay/Sure
  - Record yourself saying these 5 phrases
  - Write each phrase 3 times
- **Anki:** 5 greeting cards

### Day 16: Numbers 1-10 & Asking "How are you?"
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Learn numbers 1-10: 一, 二, 三, 四, 五, 六, 七, 八, 九, 十
  - Listen to pronunciation (ChinesePod)
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Count 1-10 aloud 10 times
  - Learn: 你好吗? (nǐ hǎo ma?) - How are you?
  - Response: 我很好 (wǒ hěn hǎo) - I'm very well
  - Shadowing: Record yourself
  - Anki: 10 number cards + 2 phrase cards

### Day 17: Polite Phrases & "My name is..."
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Learn: 请 (qǐng) - please
  - Learn: 谢谢你 (xièxie nǐ) - thank you (you)
  - Learn: 不客气 (búkèqi) - you're welcome
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Learn: 我叫... (wǒ jiào...) - My name is...
  - Learn: 很高兴认识你 (hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ) - Nice to meet you
  - Practice: Introduce yourself (record 3 times, compare to natives)
  - Dialogue practice: Simple greeting exchange
  - Anki: 6 new phrase cards + number review (5 cards)

### Day 18: Basic Questions (Age, Nationality)
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Learn: 你多大? (nǐ duōdà?) - How old are you?
  - Learn: 我...岁 (wǒ...suì) - I'm ... years old
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Learn: 你是哪国人? (nǐ shì nǎ guó rén?) - What nationality are you?
  - Learn: 我是...人 (wǒ shì...rén) - I'm ... (nationality)
  - Practice: Answer simple questions about yourself
  - Record a full greeting dialogue (5-6 exchanges)
  - Anki: 4 new question cards + 10 review

### Day 19: Family Members & Simple Descriptions
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Learn basic family: 爸爸, 妈妈, 哥哥, 妹妹, 弟弟
  - Listen to family vocabulary (ChinesePod)
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Learn: 这是... (zhè shì...) - This is...
  - Learn: 他/她很... (tā/tā hěn...) - He/She is...
  - Practice: Describe family members
  - Create sentences: "这是我爸爸，他很高" (This is my dad, he is tall)
  - Anki: 7 family cards + 8 review cards

### Day 20: Comprehensive Greeting Practice
- **Morning (20 min):**
  - Watch: Easy Chinese episode with simple greetings (3-4 min)
  - Transcribe 5 key phrases you hear
  - Shadowing: Repeat after natives
- **Evening (25 min):**
  - Full dialogue practice: Imagine meeting someone new
  - Record 8-10 exchanges covering:
    - Greeting, asking how they are
    - Introducing yourself
    - Saying nationality/age
    - Saying goodbye
  - Compare your tone/pronunciation to natives
- **Anki:** Full review (40+ cards, all categories)

### Day 21: SPEAKING CHECKPOINT TEST
- **Morning (20 min):**
  - Listening comprehension: Understand 5 simple dialogues
  - Write pinyin + English for phrases you hear
  - Target: 80%+ understanding
- **Evening (25 min):**
  - Speaking test: Record yourself in a simple dialogue
  - Have you mastered all Week 3 greetings?
  - Self-review: Can you say these naturally?
- **Anki:** Full vocabulary review (50 cards)
- **Milestone:** ✅ Confidently greet people; count 1-10; know ~30 HSK1 phrases

## Week 4: Review & Speaking Mastery (Days 22-30)
**Goal:** Integrate all learned skills; build confidence speaking

### Day 22: Tone + Character Review Day
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Tone identification quiz: Listen to 30 syllables, write pinyin
  - Target: 28/30 correct
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Character writing quiz: Write 15 characters from memory (using Hanzi Writer)
  - Radical identification: Identify radicals in 10 characters
  - Review any weak areas
- **Anki:** Full tone + character review (50 cards)

### Day 23: Vocabulary Expansion (20 New Words)
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Learn 10 new common HSK1 words:
    - 是 (shì) - to be
    - 有 (yǒu) - to have
    - 吃 (chī) - to eat
    - 喝 (hē) - to drink
    - 去 (qù) - to go
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Learn 10 more: 来 (lái), 做 (zuò), 好 (hǎo), 大 (dà), 小 (xiǎo)
  - Create sentences using each word
  - Shadowing: Listen & repeat examples
  - Anki: 20 vocabulary cards

### Day 24: Simple Sentence Building
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Review basic sentence structure: S + V + O
  - Examples: 我吃饭 (I eat rice)
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Create 15 simple sentences using learned vocabulary
  - Shadowing: Listen to native speaker examples
  - Record yourself saying your sentences
  - Anki: Practice sentence cards (15 cards)

### Day 25: Listening & Speaking Integration
- **Morning (20 min):**
  - Watch: Easy Chinese - 2 episodes (2-3 min each)
  - Transcribe key phrases
  - Identify: Tones, characters, vocabulary you've learned
- **Evening (20 min):**
  - Recreate dialogues from the videos
  - Record yourself speaking
  - Compare pronunciation/natural flow to natives
  - Anki: Vocabulary + phrase review (30 cards)

### Day 26: Comprehensive Review Day 1
- **Morning (20 min):**
  - Tone test: Identify all 4 tones in 30 syllables (90%+ target)
  - Character test: Write 20 characters from memory (90%+ target)
  - Vocabulary test: Match 25 words to English definitions
- **Evening (25 min):**
  - Speaking practice: Full dialogue recording
  - Test yourself on Week 3-4 greetings and introductions
  - Anki: Full mixed review (60 cards)

### Day 27: Comprehensive Review Day 2
- **Morning (20 min):**
  - Listen to 10 short dialogues (30 sec each)
  - Understanding test: Answer simple questions
  - Pinyin writing: Transcribe what you hear
- **Evening (25 min):**
  - Character recognition: See 20 characters, identify them
  - Sentence building: Create 10 sentences from word list
  - Shadowing: Easy Chinese video (repeat 3+ times)
  - Anki: Vocabulary review (50 cards)

### Day 28: Speaking Confidence Builder
- **Morning (15 min):**
  - Review: All greetings (say 5 times each)
  - Review: All numbers 1-10 (count forward/backward)
- **Evening (30 min):**
  - Record full introductions covering:
    1. Greeting someone
    2. Asking how they are
    3. Introducing your name
    4. Telling your age/nationality
    5. Mentioning family
    6. Saying goodbye
  - Do this 2-3 times and compare to natives
  - Focus on: Tone accuracy, natural rhythm, confidence

### Day 29: Creative Application
- **Morning (20 min):**
  - Imagine a real scenario: Meeting a Chinese person
  - Plan what you would say/ask
  - Research: Any additional words needed?
- **Evening (25 min):**
  - Create a full dialogue (8-10 exchanges, written)
  - Record yourself as both speakers
  - Anki: Full comprehensive review (70 cards)

### Day 30: 30-DAY CHECKPOINT EXAM
- **Full Assessment (45 min total):**

#### Part 1: Pinyin & Tones (10 min)
- Listen to 20 syllables → write pinyin with tone marks
- Target: 18/20 correct (90%)

#### Part 2: Character Recognition & Writing (10 min)
- Recognition: See 15 characters → identify + pronounce
- Writing: Write 10 characters from memory
- Target: 23/25 correct (92%)

#### Part 3: Vocabulary & Comprehension (10 min)
- Listen to 5 short dialogues (30-60 sec each)
- Answer 10 comprehension questions
- Target: 8/10 correct (80%+)

#### Part 4: Speaking Assessment (10 min)
- Record a dialogue covering:
  - Greeting & how are you?
  - Self-introduction
  - Questions about the other person
  - Saying goodbye
- Self-evaluate: Tone accuracy? Natural flow? Confidence?

#### Part 5: Reflection (5 min)
- Write notes: What's your strongest area?
- What needs more practice?
- Are you ready for Week 5 (Phase 2)?

## Important Reminders

1. **Consistency beats cramming** - 30 min daily > 3 hours once a week
2. **Say it out loud** - Your mouth teaches your brain faster
3. **Record yourself** - Compare to natives, catch pronunciation issues
4. **Celebrate small wins** - You're learning an entirely new system!
5. **Don't skip days** - You'll lose momentum; momentum is everything

---

## What Comes After Day 30?

Once you complete this 30-day foundation, you'll move into **Phase 2: Core Vocabulary** (Days 31-60), where you'll:
- Expand vocabulary to 100+ HSK1 words

- Practice simple conversations

You'll be amazed at how much you've accomplished. Trust the process. 💪
