# 30-Day Lessons Index - Your Complete Learning Guide

Welcome! This is your master guide to all 7 lessons created for your 30-day HSK1 foundation journey.

---

## 📚 Complete Lesson Directory

### **LESSON 1: Pinyin & The Four Tones** (Days 1-7)
**File:** `LESSON-01-Pinyin-and-Tones.md`

**What You'll Learn:**
- What pinyin is and why it's essential
- All 4 tones (High, Rising, Dipping, Falling)
- Tone mark placement rules
- Basic tone pronunciation practice

**Key Vocabulary:** mā, má, mǎ, mà (the famous "MA" example)

**Duration:** ~3-4 hours over Week 1

**Status:** ✅ Complete

---

### **LESSON 2: Radicals 101** (Days 8-14)
**File:** `LESSON-02-Radicals-101.md`

**What You'll Learn:**
- What radicals are and how they work
- The 5 most common radicals:
  - 人 (person)
  - 木 (wood)
  - 水 (water)
  - 火 (fire)
  - 心 (heart)
- How radicals hint at character meaning
- Character recognition patterns

**Key Skills:** Identifying radicals, grouping characters, using radicals for memory

**Duration:** ~3-4 hours over Week 2

**Status:** ✅ Complete

---

### **LESSON 3: Basic Greetings** (Days 15-21)
**File:** `LESSON-03-Basic-Greetings.md`

**What You'll Learn:**
- 10 essential greetings:
  - 你好 (hello)
  - 谢谢 (thank you)
  - 对不起 (sorry)
  - 再见 (goodbye)
  - And 6 more!
- When to use each greeting
- Natural pronunciation with tones
- Simple greeting dialogue

**Key Skills:** Speaking naturally, tone accuracy, basic conversation

**Duration:** ~3-4 hours over Week 3

**Status:** ✅ Complete

---

### **LESSON 4: Numbers 1-10** (Days 16-23)
**File:** `LESSON-04-Numbers-1-to-10.md`

**What You'll Learn:**
- All numbers 1-10 in Mandarin
- Special pronunciation rules (一 changes tone!)
- Using numbers in sentences:
  - Age: 我五岁 (I'm 5 years old)
  - Quantities: 三个朋友 (3 friends)
  - Phone numbers: 一三五 (1-3-5)
- Classifier system (个)

**Key Vocabulary:** 一, 二, 三, 四, 五, 六, 七, 八, 九, 十

**Duration:** ~2-3 hours (bonus lesson within Week 3)

**Status:** ✅ Complete

---

### **LESSON 5: Core Verbs & Sentence Structure** (Days 22-30)
**File:** `LESSON-05-Core-Verbs-and-Sentence-Structure.md`

**What You'll Learn:**
- Chinese sentence structure (SVO: Subject-Verb-Object)
- 5 essential verbs:
  - 是 (to be)
  - 有 (to have)
  - 吃 (to eat)
  - 喝 (to drink)
  - 去 (to go)
- How to build simple sentences
- Question formation with 吗
- Negation rules (不 vs 没有)

**Key Skills:** Sentence building, creating statements and questions, basic conversation

**Duration:** ~4-5 hours over Week 4

**Status:** ✅ Complete

---

### **LESSON 6: Family Members & Descriptions** (Days 22-30)
**File:** `LESSON-06-Family-Members-and-Basic-Descriptions.md`

**What You'll Learn:**
- 12+ family member vocabulary words:
  - 爸爸, 妈妈, 哥哥, 妹妹, 姐姐, 弟弟
  - Extended family: 爷爷, 奶奶, 外公, 外婆
- Basic adjectives for description:
  - 好, 大, 小, 高, 年轻, 开心, 聪明
- How to describe people and family
- Cultural notes on Chinese family hierarchy

**Key Skills:** Talking about family, describing people, understanding relationships

**Duration:** ~3-4 hours over Week 4

**Status:** ✅ Complete

---

### **LESSON 7: 30-Day Review & Integration** (Day 30)
**File:** `LESSON-07-30-Day-Review-and-Integration.md`

**What You'll Learn:**
- Comprehensive review of all 6 lessons
- 30-Day self-assessment checklist
- Speaking test (final evaluation)
- Transition guide to Phase 2
- Sustainable learning habits
- Everything you've accomplished

**Key Skills:** Self-evaluation, progress tracking, preparation for Phase 2

**Duration:** ~2-3 hours (completion assessment)

**Status:** ✅ Complete

- Other lessons as supplementary

**Week 2: Master Characters**
- LESSON-02 (complete)
- Character practice 15 min/day

**Week 3: Master Speaking**
- LESSON-03 (complete)
- LESSON-04 (numbers)
- Speaking practice 20 min/day
- LESSON-01 + LESSON-02 (review 5 min/day)

**Week 4: Master Sentences & Integration**
- LESSON-05 (verbs)
- LESSON-06 (family)
- LESSON-07 (assessment)
- All previous lessons (review 10 min/day)

**For learners with language experience**

**Time commitment:** 30 min/day, 2 weeks
