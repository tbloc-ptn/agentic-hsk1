# Learning Resources for HSK1

## High-Quality Resources

### Pinyin & Tone System
- **[New Practical Chinese Reader (NPCR) Series](https://chineselessons.com/)** — The gold standard textbook for beginners. Excellent coverage of pinyin, tones, and cultural context.
- **[Mandarin.com Tone Tutorial](https://www.mandarin.com/beginner/tones)** — Interactive tone practice with audio examples.
- **[The Pinyin Song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x8gN1wd3Euw)** — Memorable introduction to the pinyin system.

### HSK1 Vocabulary & Lists
- **[Official HSK Vocabulary List](https://www.chineselearning.org/hsk1-vocabulary/)** — The authoritative ~150 word list for HSK1.
- **[Pleco App](https://www.plecodict.com/)** — Comprehensive Chinese dictionary with HSK level filtering.
- **[Anki HSK1 Deck](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/1644643155)** — Spaced repetition flashcards for HSK1 vocabulary.

### Character Learning
- **[Hanzi Writer](https://www.hanziwriter.org/)** — Interactive character stroke order practice.
- **[Making Sense of Chinese Characters by Dr. Michael Everson](https://www.irisu.org/cjk/)** — Learn how characters are constructed from radicals.

### Listening Practice
- **[ChinesePod - HSK1 Course](https://www.chinesepod.com/)** — Audio-based lessons with native speakers.
- **[CCTV Learn Chinese](https://learnchinese.cctv.com/)** — Official Chinese media with lesson plans.
- **[YouTube - Easy Chinese Channel](https://www.youtube.com/c/EasyChinese)** — Short, authentic videos with subtitles.

### Practice Tests & Exams
- **[HSK Online Test](https://www.hsktest.com/)** — Official HSK testing organization practice exams.
- **[DuChinese](https://www.duolinese.com/)** — Interactive lessons with progress tracking.
- **[Busuu - Chinese Course](https://www.busuu.com/)** — Structured HSK preparation with community feedback.

### Writing Practice
- **[Skritter](https://skritter.com/)** — Interactive handwriting practice for characters and words.
- **[Chinese Textbook - HSK1](https://www.amazon.com/Modern-Chinese-Elementary-Student-Textbook/dp/7887310667/)** — Print workbooks with writing exercises.
